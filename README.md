## 🚀 Live Citizen & Examination Tools

1. **[SSO Photo & Signature Resizer](https://rajsso.com/RajssoTools/sso-photo-crop-resizer/)**
   - Automatically crop and resize passport photos (50KB-100KB) and signatures (20KB-50KB) according to RPSC and RSSB recruitment specifications.
2. **[SSO Document Image Resizer](https://rajsso.com/RajssoTools/sso-document-image-resizer/)**
   - Resize caste certificates, domicile certificates, marksheets, and identity proofs for e-Mitra and SSO uploads.
3. **[OTR Fee & Eligibility Calculator](https://rajsso.com/RajssoTools/sso-otr-fee-eligibility-calculator/)**
   - Calculate exact government one-time registration fees (General/OBC Creamy: ₹600 vs Non-Creamy/SC/ST/EWS/PwD: ₹400).
4. **[Exam Age Eligibility Calculator](https://rajsso.com/RajssoTools/sso-exam-age-eligibility-calculator/)**
   - Calculate candidate age and relaxation on official recruitment cutoff dates.
5. **[SSO Password Generator & Policy Validator](https://rajsso.com/RajssoTools/sso-password-generator-validator/)**
   - Generate secure, compliant passwords satisfying Rajasthan DoIT&C security standards.
